    Key terms

    Diploid

    A cell with two homologous sets of chromosomes, one set inherited from each parent. Diploid is written as 2n.

    Haploid

    A cell with one set of chromosomes. Secondary spermatocytes, spermatids, and spermatozoa are haploid, written as n.

    Spermiogenesis

    The differentiation of haploid spermatids into spermatozoa. Spermiogenesis does not include another meiotic division.

    Spermiation

    The release of spermatozoa from Sertoli cells into the lumen of the seminiferous tubule.
